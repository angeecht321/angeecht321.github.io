## 📌 About this project

This platform helps retail businesses track products from warehouses to store shelves. It uses data from over one million retail records to show you where your inventory sits and how your business performs. The system combines sales figures and supply chain patterns to provide a clear view of your operations.

You gain access to automated reporting without needing to write code. This tool cleans raw data and organizes it into charts that help you make better business choices.

## 🛠 Features

*   **Sales Tracking:** See current performance against past sales.
*   **Inventory Oversight:** Monitor stock levels to prevent shortages.
*   **Warehouse Monitoring:** Track how products move through your supply chain.
*   **Automated Data Cleanup:** Process messy data without manual input.
*   **Visual Reports:** View your business status through clear dashboards.
*   **Forecast Models:** Prepare for future demand based on past trends.

## 📈 Understanding your dashboard

The dashboard organizes your business data into sections. Use the tabs at the top of your screen to switch between views.

### The supply chain view
This section shows a map of your warehouses. You see which items have high stock levels and which items need reordering. Items in red indicate a stock shortage.

### The sales report view
Use this view to see revenue trends. You can filter data by date, product category, or store location. The charts update as you select different filters.

### The predictive model view
This section shows forecasts for the next month. The system uses past data to guess future demand. Use these estimates to adjust your stock orders.

## 🛠 Troubleshooting common issues

*   **The application will not open:** Check if your antivirus software is blocking the application. Create an exception for the installation folder if necessary.
*   **The dashboard shows no data:** Check your internet connection. The system needs to verify account credentials to sync the initial record set.
*   **The charts look distorted:** Ensure your Windows display scaling is set to 100% or 125% in the system settings.
*   **The program runs slow:** Close other heavy programs like web browsers or video editors. Large data sets require system memory to load correctly.
